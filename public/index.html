SHATSU STARTER GUIDE

Listen together in minutes ♫

No server setup, no signup, no complicated steps.

🎧

1. Create your space

Type any nickname and tap Create room. Your friends can join using your room link or 8-character code.

💌

2. Invite your friends

Inside the room, tap Invite friend to open your phone's share menu. Or tap Copy to send the invite link yourself.

▶

3. Choose a song

Choose Audius · Free in Music Library, search by song or artist, then tap Play or + Queue. No sign-in or API key needed. YouTube links work too; searching the YouTube catalog by name still needs Google's key.

🔄

4. Stay in sync

The host controls playback. If your phone blocks autoplay, tap Tap to start listening. Use the ↻ button to catch up with the room.

🎵

5. Spotify is optional

Spotify needs an eligible Premium account and developer authorization. Tap Brave ↗ to sign in in your browser. Spotify songs play through each person's own Spotify app. Automatic matching across Spotify and YouTube is not available yet.
